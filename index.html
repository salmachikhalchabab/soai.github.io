About the Competition

Discover the mission, vision, and impact of the Syrian Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence

The Syrian Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (SOAI) is a national competition that challenges and inspires the next generation of Syrian AI innovators.

Designed for exceptional young Syrian minds, SOAI provides a unique platform to showcase talent, solve real-world problems, and build a thriving AI community in Syria.

Key Objectives

  • Promote AI education and research among Syrian pre-university students
  • Identify and nurture exceptional Syrian talent in artificial intelligence
  • Foster collaboration between Syrian students and global AI experts
  • Explore ethical implications and responsible development of AI

Competition Focus Areas

Machine Learning

Algorithms, neural networks, and predictive modeling challenges

Neural Networks

Deep learning architectures and training methodologies

NLP

Language processing, generation, and understanding tasks

Computer Vision

Image recognition, processing, and analysis problems

About IOAI

The International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence — the global stage SOAI prepares you for

The International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI) is a premier global competition for high school students, aiming to cultivate both a strong theoretical foundation and hands-on expertise in Artificial Intelligence.

Each year, talented young minds from around the world come together to compete, collaborate, and explore the frontiers of AI. The Syrian Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (SOAI) is Syria's national pathway to discover exceptional students and prepare them to represent the country on this international stage.

IOAI 2026 will be held in Kazakhstan. Registration is now open on the official website.
